Meh

Just another image editor. Whatever.

Microsoft Store soonMac App Store soon

Free for Windows and Mac. No account, no ads, and no data collection.

Fast and light

No splash screen. No spinner. Just the photo.

Meh is native code that draws with your graphics card. Start it with a 10-megapixel photo, and the photo is on screen in about a fifth of a second. Then Meh uses no CPU until you do something.

  • 0.2 sfrom start to a 10-megapixel photo on screen
  • < 6 MBto download, for Arm or x64 PCs
  • 0 %CPU while you look at a photo
  • 120 Hzsmooth panning on fast displays

Crop

Drag a box. Press Enter.

Everything outside the box goes dark, and a grid helps you line things up. Choose 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, or 16:9, or type exact numbers. The cut-off part stays until you save, so Undo can bring it back.

Rotate, flip, and resize

Sideways photo? Fixed.

Ctrl+R and Ctrl+L turn the image, and Flip mirrors it. Both are instant. Resize keeps the proportions. It uses a sharp filter for photos, or a crisp one for pixel art and screenshots.

Ctrl+R turns it rightFlip mirrors itResize to 1428 × 952 px

Mark up

Circle it. Point at it. Write on it.

Pen, highlighter, boxes, ellipses, lines, arrows, text, and emoji stickers. Each mark stays a separate object, so you can move it, restyle it, or delete it until you save.

PenHighlighterTextArrowEmoji stickerEvery mark stays editable until you save

Paste and copy

Paste it, fix it, copy it back.

Ctrl+V drops a copied image on top of the open one, where you can move it and resize it. With nothing open, the image gets its own tab. Ctrl+C copies the result, marks and all, ready for a chat or an email.

Measure

How big is that, exactly?

Drag across anything to read its length and its angle in pixels. Tell Meh one length that you know, and it measures in centimeters, inches, or any unit you type. Measurements stay out of the saved file, unless you burn them in.

Stitch

Two images, one picture.

Put two or more images side by side or stacked. Match their sizes, add a gap, and check the live preview before you make it. Your marks come along, still editable.

Undo

Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z.

Every step can be undone and redone: marks, crops, turns, and resizes. And Meh asks before it closes an image with unsaved changes.

Save

See the file size before you save.

Save in place, or as PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, or BMP. The size estimate follows the quality slider. Keep the photo details, or leave out the location. If a format would drop something, such as transparency, Meh tells you first. A failed save leaves your original as it was.

Light and dark

Light, dark, or whatever your system says.

The grays stay neutral, so your photo is the only color on the screen. Meh follows the system theme, or you can choose one in Settings.
