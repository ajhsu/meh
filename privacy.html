Meh privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026. Applies to Meh for Windows and Meh for macOS, from AJ.H.

The short version

Meh does not collect, store, or send any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Your images

Meh opens and saves the files that you choose, on your device. It does not upload them anywhere. When you copy or paste, the image goes through your system clipboard, which other apps on your device can read.

Photo details

When you save, Meh can keep the details stored in a photo, such as the camera, the date, and the place where it was taken. You control this in the save options and in Settings. "Remove location data" leaves the place out of the saved file.

Settings

Meh keeps your settings, such as the theme and the size of a new image, in a small file on your device. On macOS, the sandboxed app also keeps the folder permissions that you grant, so it can browse the other images in a folder. These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes them, or you can delete them yourself.

Children

Meh collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new version and its date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ajhsu/meh/issues.
